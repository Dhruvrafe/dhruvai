The Problem
MANUAL ROUTING
DOESN'T SCALE

Every day, travel queries land in your CRM from people excited about a trip. Each one needs to reach the right agent — someone who knows the destination, has the right relationships, and can convert it. Right now, that's happening manually, or not at all.

Speed. A query sitting unassigned is a lead going cold. That excitement has a shelf life. Every hour it sits in a pile is an hour your competitor is calling them instead.

Accuracy. People don't fill forms cleanly. Someone wanting Bhutan types "Thimpu" or "butan." Someone booking a cruise selects India as their region because that's where they're departing from, not where they're going. A distracted human gets this wrong.

Consistency. Your routing logic lives in someone's head. When that person leaves, the logic goes with them.

The Solution
AI THAT READS,
ROUTES, REASONS

The moment a query lands, the system reads every field — free-text location, region dropdown, country, trip type — and understands what the person actually means, even with spelling errors or city names instead of countries.

It then checks your routing rules, written in plain English, and assigns the query to the right agent instantly. No human in the loop, no delay, no misrouting.

The system has also analysed each agent's historical behaviour and performance data — close rates by destination, conversion speed, current workload — so it routes based on who will actually win the lead, not just who's next in queue.

Rules live in one place you control. Change two words and every future query routes differently. No IT ticket, no developer needed.

How It Works — Click Each Step
01
Lead Enters CRM
A travel enquiry lands — from a web form, ad, WhatsApp, or manual entry. The raw data is logged into Zoho CRM immediately, triggering the routing workflow.
02
AI Interprets the Query
The system reads all fields — destination text, region dropdown, travel type, trip duration, group size. It resolves ambiguities: "Thimpu" → Bhutan, "south of france" → French Riviera. Misspellings, shorthand, and partial info are handled.
03
Routing Rules Applied
Plain-English routing rules are checked against the interpreted query. Kiran handles Sikkim and Northeast India. Rahul owns all Southeast Asia. Priya manages Europe luxury. Rules are centrally managed and instantly updated.
04
Agent Performance Analysed
The system pulls each eligible agent's historical data: close rate for that destination type, average response time, current open leads, and recent conversion performance. The lead goes to whoever is most likely to convert it.
05
Assigned + Notified Instantly
The best agent is assigned in Zoho CRM with the full routing rationale logged. The agent receives an instant notification — WhatsApp or email — with a lead briefing so they can call within minutes.

The Problem
AN HOUR OF WORK
PER ENQUIRY

Travel agents and DMCs are constantly fielding enquiries — "7 days in Japan for a family of 4 in April," or "a romantic Bhutan trip under a certain budget." At the same time, suppliers are sending dense, unformatted itinerary emails with pricing, day plans, and logistics all jumbled in plain text.

Converting either input — a vague client request or a raw supplier email — into a polished, client-ready PDF presentation meant hours of manual work in Word or Canva every single time.

Multiply that by 20 enquiries a day and you have an operations bottleneck that caps how many leads you can even respond to.

The Solution
PASTE ANYTHING.
GET A PRESENTATION.

Whether you're starting from a client WhatsApp message or a supplier's raw email, you paste whatever text you have and the AI structures everything: destination, day-by-day itinerary, highlights, pricing, inclusions, exclusions, and meal plans.

If input is vague, the AI intelligently fills in a logical itinerary around what's been requested. If it's a detailed supplier email, it extracts everything precisely. Destination photos are pulled automatically — no manual image sourcing.

Before anything reaches the client, the agent gets a full review screen to check and edit every detail. Once approved, the system produces a professionally designed A4 landscape PDF: cover page, programme summary, day-by-day layout, and pricing breakdown.

The layout is fully intelligent — no section ever cuts across a page, no page ever looks half-empty.

How It Works — Click Each Step
01
Raw Input Received
Agent pastes any text — a client's WhatsApp enquiry, a vague brief, a supplier's itinerary email, or a rough trip outline. No formatting required.
02
AI Parses & Structures
The AI reads the input and extracts destination, dates, guest count, budget tier, and travel style. For vague inputs, it makes intelligent assumptions based on the trip type and destination norms.
03
Day-by-Day Itinerary Built
Generates a complete day-by-day plan — activities, meals, logistics, hotel recommendations. Each day has a title, highlight experiences, and a narrative description. Evocative trip title and subtitle generated automatically.
04
Photos & Pricing Assembled
Destination-specific and day-specific photos are sourced automatically. Pricing, inclusions, exclusions, and meal plans are formatted into structured sections ready for the PDF.
05
Agent Reviews & Edits
Before anything reaches the client, the agent sees a complete review screen. Every detail — day titles, descriptions, dates, pricing, meals — can be edited. Full control retained throughout.
06
Branded PDF Generated
A professional A4 landscape PDF is produced: full-bleed cover with your company logo, programme summary, highlights, rich day-by-day layout, and a clean pricing breakdown. Smart pagination ensures nothing looks broken or half-empty.

The Problem
REVENUE SITTING
IN DEAD DATA

Every travel company has hundreds of leads that went cold — people who were genuinely interested, contacted you, but didn't book. They're sitting in your CRM marked as "Lost" or "Not Interested," and nobody touches them again.

The notes your sales team left tell you exactly why they died. Budget was tight. Travel timing didn't work. They couldn't decide between two destinations. Life got in the way. This information is gold — but nobody is using it.

Most businesses spend money acquiring new leads while completely ignoring a warmer, cheaper, and already-informed audience sitting in their existing data.

The Solution
AI READS THE NOTES.
BUILDS THE PITCH.

The system reads the notes field of every dead lead in your CRM and uses AI to categorise each one into a segment based on why it died. Budget issue, timing mismatch, destination indecision, competitor lost, or simply went quiet.

For each segment, it builds a hyper-specific re-engagement strategy: a budget-sensitive lead gets pitched a budget-aligned destination or a payment plan. A timing-dead lead gets contacted when their season opens up. A competitor-lost lead gets shown what makes your service different.

The output is ready-to-send: email draft, WhatsApp message, and an AI call script — all personalised to that lead's specific context, not a generic blast.

How It Works — Click Each Step
01
CRM Notes Exported
All dead/lost leads from Zoho CRM are extracted. The key input is the notes field — whatever the sales rep documented about why the lead didn't convert.
02
AI Reads & Categorises
AI reads each lead's notes and assigns a segment: Budget Constrained, Timing Mismatch, Destination Undecided, Competitor Lost, No Response, or Life Event. Each segment gets a different revival strategy.
03
Revival Strategy Generated
For each segment, the system builds a campaign: what to offer, what angle to use, and what message will resonate. Budget leads get affordable alternatives. Timing leads get contacted when their window opens. Undecided leads get a curated comparison.
04
Personalised Content Drafted
AI drafts a personalised email, WhatsApp message, and AI call script for each lead — referencing their specific destination interest, the objection they raised, and a tailored offer addressing it directly.
05
Campaign Deployed
Campaigns are pushed back into Zoho CRM and triggered on schedule. Re-engaged leads re-enter the active pipeline. Responses feed back into AI analysis for continuous improvement.
