The Problem
MANUAL ROUTING
DOES NOT SCALE

Every day, travel queries land in your CRM from people excited about a trip. Each one needs to reach the right agent, someone who knows the destination and can convert it. Right now that is happening manually, or not at all.

Speed. A query sitting unassigned is a lead going cold. That excitement has a shelf life. Every hour it sits in a pile is an hour your competitor is calling them instead.

Accuracy. People do not fill forms cleanly. Someone wanting Bhutan types "Thimpu" or "butan." A distracted human gets this wrong, especially when handling 50 leads a day.

Consistency. Your routing logic lives in someone's head. When that person leaves, the logic goes with them.

The Solution
AI THAT READS,
ROUTES, REASONS

The moment a query lands, the system reads every field, free text location, region dropdown, trip type, and understands what the person actually means, even with spelling errors or city names instead of countries.

It checks your routing rules, written in plain English, and assigns the query to the right agent instantly. No human in the loop, no delay, no misrouting.

The system has also analysed each agent's historical performance: close rates by destination, conversion speed, current workload. The lead goes to whoever will actually win it.

Rules live in one place you control. Change two words and every future query routes differently. No IT ticket needed.

The Problem
AN HOUR OF WORK
PER ENQUIRY

Travel agents and DMCs are constantly fielding enquiries: "7 days in Japan for a family of 4 in April" or "a romantic Bhutan trip under a certain budget." At the same time, suppliers send dense unformatted itinerary emails with pricing, day plans, and logistics all jumbled in plain text.

Converting either input into a polished client-ready PDF meant hours of manual work in Word or Canva every single time. Multiply that by 20 enquiries a day and you have a bottleneck that caps how many leads you can even respond to.

The Solution
PASTE ANYTHING.
GET A PRESENTATION.

Paste any text, a client WhatsApp message, a vague brief, a supplier's raw email, and AI structures everything automatically: destination, day-by-day itinerary, highlights, pricing, inclusions, exclusions, and meal plans.

If input is vague, AI intelligently fills in a logical itinerary. If it is a detailed supplier email, it extracts everything precisely. Destination photos are sourced automatically.

Before anything reaches the client, the agent reviews and edits every detail. Once approved, a professionally designed A4 landscape PDF is generated with smart pagination. Nothing cuts across a page, no page looks half-empty.

How It Works, Hover Each Step
01
Raw Input Received
Agent pastes any text: a client WhatsApp enquiry, a vague brief, or a supplier's itinerary email. No formatting required whatsoever.
02
AI Parses and Structures
AI extracts destination, dates, guest count, budget tier, and travel style. For vague inputs it makes intelligent assumptions based on trip type and destination norms.
03
Day-by-Day Itinerary Built
A complete day-by-day plan is generated: activities, meals, logistics, hotel recommendations. Each day has a title, highlight experiences, and a narrative description. Evocative trip title and subtitle created automatically.
04
Photos and Pricing Assembled
Destination-specific photos are sourced automatically. Pricing, inclusions, exclusions, and meal plans are formatted into structured sections ready for the PDF.
05
Agent Reviews and Edits
Before anything reaches the client, the agent sees a complete review screen. Every detail can be edited. Full control is retained throughout the process.
06
Branded PDF Generated
A professional A4 landscape PDF is produced: full-bleed cover with company logo, programme summary, highlights section, rich day-by-day layout, and a clean pricing breakdown. Smart pagination throughout.

The Problem
REVENUE SITTING
IN DEAD DATA

Every travel company has hundreds of leads that went cold, people who were genuinely interested, contacted you, but did not book. They sit in your CRM marked as Lost and nobody touches them again.

The notes your sales team left tell you exactly why they died. Budget was tight. Travel timing did not work. They could not choose between destinations. This information is gold and nobody is using it.

Most businesses spend money acquiring new leads while completely ignoring a warmer, cheaper, and already-informed audience sitting in their existing data.

The Solution
AI READS THE NOTES.
BUILDS THE PITCH.

The system reads the notes field of every dead lead and categorises each one by why it died. Budget issue, timing mismatch, destination indecision, competitor lost, or simply went quiet.

For each segment, it builds a hyper-specific re-engagement strategy. Budget leads get pitched a budget-aligned destination. Timing leads get contacted when their season opens. Undecided leads get a curated comparison.

The output is ready to send: email draft, WhatsApp message, and an AI call script, all personalised to that lead's specific context, not a generic blast.
